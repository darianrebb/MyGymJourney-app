Support

Thanks for using My Gym Journey.

Get help

Questions, bugs, or feedback? Email support@mygymjourney.app.

Please include:

  • Your device model (e.g. iPhone 15)
  • iOS version
  • App version
  • A short description of the issue

Workouts & exercises

Create exercises in the Exercises tab, add them to workouts, then tap Play to start a session. You can also start today’s scheduled workout from the Home Screen quick action.

Sharing

Export workouts or exercises as .mygymjourney files via Share, and import them from another device. Imports always create a copy and never overwrite your existing items.

Apple Health

Completed workouts can be saved to Apple Health when Health access is enabled. Manage permissions in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

Optional donations

In-app donations in Settings are optional and do not unlock features. They’re consumable tip purchases that support ongoing development.

Features Summary

Reopen the Features Summary anytime from Settings → Help → Show Features Summary.

See our Privacy Policy for how the app handles data.
